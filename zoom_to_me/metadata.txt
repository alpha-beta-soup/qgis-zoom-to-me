description=One button that zooms the map to where this computer appears to be.
about=Adds a toolbar button that centres the map on your current location. If QGIS has a live GPS connection (Settings > Options > GPS, or the GPS Information panel) its latest fix is used. Otherwise the plugin asks https://ipinfo.io where your public IP address is, which is only accurate to roughly the city level and will be wrong behind a VPN or corporate gateway. Note that using the fallback sends your public IP address to ipinfo.io, a third-party service.
